# INC-0010: Worker memory growth and OOM kills (synthetic)

> Synthetic incident written for the EvidenceDesk benchmark. Services, people and events are fictional.

## Summary

notifications-worker pods were OOM-killed every few hours.

## Impact

Delayed notifications during restarts; no data loss.

## Timeline (UTC)

- Day 1 OOM kills begin after a release adding template caching.
- Day 1 Mitigation: `--limit-max-requests 5000` to recycle Uvicorn workers.
- Day 3 Heap profile shows an unbounded per-process template cache.
- Day 4 Fix deployed; recycling setting removed.

## Root cause

A new in-process cache had no size limit and grew with every distinct template variant.

## Resolution

Replaced the cache with an LRU cache of 512 entries.

## Follow-ups

- Treat `--limit-max-requests` as a mitigation, not a fix; track it in the backlog.
