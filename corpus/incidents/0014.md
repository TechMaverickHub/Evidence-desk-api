# INC-0014: Welcome emails silently not sent (synthetic)

> Synthetic incident written for the EvidenceDesk benchmark. Services, people and events are fictional.

## Summary

New users stopped receiving welcome emails, but sign-up responses were 200.

## Impact

About 2,000 users received no welcome email over 3 days.

## Timeline (UTC)

- Day 1 Email provider rotates API keys.
- Day 4 Marketing notices zero welcome emails.
- Day 4 Logs show exceptions in a background task.

## Root cause

Emails were sent with `BackgroundTasks`, which run after the response is sent. The task raised an authentication error that was only logged; the client had already received 200 and nothing retried.

## Resolution

Emails now go through a durable job queue with retries and an alert on failures.

## Follow-ups

- Use background tasks only for work that may be lost.
