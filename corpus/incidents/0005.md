# INC-0005: Partner webhooks rejected with extra_forbidden (synthetic)

> Synthetic incident written for the EvidenceDesk benchmark. Services, people and events are fictional.

## Summary

A partner's webhook calls to billing-gateway started failing with 422 after we tightened request validation.

## Impact

Payment status updates from one partner were delayed for 3 hours.

## Timeline (UTC)

- 10:00 Release sets `model_config = ConfigDict(extra='forbid')` on webhook models.
- 10:05 Partner webhooks fail with validation error type `extra_forbidden`.
- 12:50 Partner support asks why every call fails.
- 13:05 Rollback of the strict model.

## Root cause

The partner sends an undocumented `trace_id` field. Forbidding extra fields turned that harmless field into a validation error.

## Resolution

Webhook models use `extra='ignore'`; strict models are kept for internal APIs only.

## Follow-ups

- Agree a schema-change policy with partners before tightening validation.
