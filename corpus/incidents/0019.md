# INC-0019: Server-sent events delivered late (synthetic)

> Synthetic incident written for the EvidenceDesk benchmark. Services, people and events are fictional.

## Summary

Dashboard live updates, sent as a streaming response, arrived in bursts every few seconds instead of immediately.

## Impact

Degraded live view for operators for a week; no data loss.

## Timeline (UTC)

- Week 1 Release adds `GZipMiddleware` to the whole application.
- Week 2 Operators report delayed live updates.
- Week 2 Streaming route excluded from compression.

## Root cause

Compression batched the small event messages before sending them, so events were held back until enough bytes accumulated.

## Resolution

The live-events route is served by a separate app without `GZipMiddleware`.

## Follow-ups

- Test streaming endpoints with every middleware that wraps responses.
