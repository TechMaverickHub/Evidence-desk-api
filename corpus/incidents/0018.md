# INC-0018: Settings object mutation crashed the scheduler (synthetic)

> Synthetic incident written for the EvidenceDesk benchmark. Services, people and events are fictional.

## Summary

The nightly scheduler crashed when a job tried to change a field on the shared settings model.

## Impact

Nightly reports were missing for one day.

## Timeline (UTC)

- Day 1 02:00 Scheduler crashes with ValidationError 'Instance is frozen'.
- Day 1 09:00 Investigated; job code mutates settings.
- Day 1 11:00 Fix deployed; reports re-run.

## Root cause

The settings model had `frozen=True` in its `model_config`, added earlier to stop accidental changes. A job written later assigned to `settings.report_date`.

## Resolution

The job now creates a copy with `settings.model_copy(update=...)`.

## Follow-ups

- Keep shared configuration immutable; pass per-job values explicitly.
