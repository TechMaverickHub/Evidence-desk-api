# INC-0008: In-flight exports cut off during deploys (synthetic)

> Synthetic incident written for the EvidenceDesk benchmark. Services, people and events are fictional.

## Summary

Long-running CSV export requests were terminated mid-stream whenever reports-api was redeployed.

## Impact

About 150 exports failed per deploy; users had to restart them.

## Timeline (UTC)

- Week 1 Support tickets about truncated CSV files.
- Week 2 Correlated with deploy times.
- Week 2 Fix deployed.

## Root cause

Pods received SIGTERM and Uvicorn shut down without waiting long enough for open requests, because `--timeout-graceful-shutdown` was not set and the orchestrator's grace period was 10 seconds.

## Resolution

Set `--timeout-graceful-shutdown 60`, raised the orchestrator grace period to 75 s, and the load balancer now drains connections before the pod is stopped.

## Follow-ups

- Move exports to background jobs with downloadable results.
