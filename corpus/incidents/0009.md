# INC-0009: Intermittent 502s from idle keep-alive connections (synthetic)

> Synthetic incident written for the EvidenceDesk benchmark. Services, people and events are fictional.

## Summary

Roughly 0.3% of requests to search-svc failed with 502 at random times, mostly after quiet periods.

## Impact

Low but constant error rate for two weeks.

## Timeline (UTC)

- Week 1 Error budget alert for search-svc.
- Week 2 Packet captures show the load balancer reusing connections Uvicorn closed.
- Week 2 Fix deployed.

## Root cause

Uvicorn closed idle keep-alive connections after its default `--timeout-keep-alive` of 5 seconds, while the load balancer kept idle upstream connections for 60 seconds. The balancer sometimes sent a request on a connection the server had just closed.

## Resolution

Set `--timeout-keep-alive 65`, longer than the load balancer's idle timeout.

## Follow-ups

- Document timeout ordering: client < load balancer < application server.
