# INC-0016: 503 responses during traffic burst (synthetic)

> Synthetic incident written for the EvidenceDesk benchmark. Services, people and events are fictional.

## Summary

A marketing campaign caused orders-api to answer 503 Service Unavailable while CPU usage stayed below 40%.

## Impact

8% of requests failed for 20 minutes during the campaign launch.

## Timeline (UTC)

- 18:00 Campaign email sent.
- 18:02 503 rate rises; CPU low.
- 18:20 Setting changed and rolled out.

## Root cause

Uvicorn ran with `--limit-concurrency 20`, copied from a load-test configuration. Above 20 concurrent connections per worker, new requests were rejected with 503.

## Resolution

Removed the limit and rely on autoscaling plus load-balancer queueing.

- Review server flags copied from load tests before production use.
